## Executive Summary

### Overview
mise bridges the enterprise capability gap for independent small business proprietors—from neighborhood panaderías and West African bistros to boutique streetwear shops and barbershops. While enterprise retailers leverage multi-million dollar supply chain algorithms to hedge against lead times and weather shifts, independent merchants are forced to rely on gut feeling, naive moving averages, and late-night manual tallies. This operational friction results in trapped working capital, painful rush-delivery surcharges, and lost sales during peak demand windows.

mise delivers an autonomous, continuous-review inventory control engine packaged in an accessible, mobile-first interface. By ingesting point-of-sale sales velocity, supplier delivery records (via camera OCR or EDI/email parsers), and hyper-local contextual feeds (weather forecasts, street closures, festivals, and cultural holidays), mise continuously calculates dynamic safety stock levels and optimal reorder points. When replenishment thresholds are breached, mise autonomously drafts and schedules purchase orders, alerting the proprietor via plain-English text notifications and a gentle reminder 48 hours before dispatch. Every order sits in a mandatory 7-day preview window, so the proprietor can review, change, reschedule, or cancel it before it is sent.

Designed explicitly with a 'Zero-IT' ethos, mise removes all technical friction. It requires no statistical jargon, no database configuration, and no expensive onboarding consultants. It meets merchants wherever they are—on messy kitchen counters, behind barber chairs, or in boutique stockrooms—with multilingual voice logging, receipt photo scanning, and conversational approvals.

### Problem Statement
Independent small business owners spend up to 15 hours a week acting as unpaid supply chain managers—manually reconciling paper delivery invoices, counting stock after midnight, and guessing order volumes. Because existing inventory tools rely on simplistic moving averages or complex enterprise ERP architectures requiring dedicated IT teams, merchants routinely oscillate between cash-draining overstocking and catastrophic stockouts during high-revenue weekend rushes.

### Proposed Solution
An intelligent, zero-configuration inventory replenishment engine that continuously monitors POS sales velocity, contextual demand drivers (weather, local events, holidays), and supplier lead times to calculate dynamic safety stock and automatically stage supplier purchase orders a full week before dispatch, with full human-in-the-loop review, reschedule, and undo capabilities.

### Target Audience
Primary: Independent restaurateurs, artisan bakers, boutique apparel curators, and service-based retail operators (barbers, stylists) generating $150k-$2.5M in annual gross merchandise volume without dedicated IT or operations staff. Secondary: Immigrant, Black, and family-owned legacy businesses that operate in bilingual or physical-first environments using low-friction devices (smartphones, tablets).

### Key Benefits
- Reclaim 8-12 hours weekly previously lost to midnight manual inventory counting, spreadsheet reconciliation, and supplier phone calls.
- Reduce perishable ingredient and seasonal retail stockouts by 40-60% while cutting emergency rush-freight surcharges.
- Protect working capital and payroll by preventing over-purchasing through continuous-review safety stock formulas and cash-flow impact simulations.
- Zero-IT barrier to entry: operational within 5 minutes via Square/Clover/Shopify sync, receipt camera snapshots, or conversational voice/SMS.

**mise**

mise is a zero-IT inventory intelligence and autonomous replenishment web application for independent small business owners (restaurateurs, boutique retailers, bakers, and barbers). It connects POS transaction velocity with hyper-local contextual signals (weather shifts, municipal event permits, seasonal holidays) to dynamically compute continuous-review safety stocks and execute automated purchase order drafting with an SMS/app 7-day preview and 'Undo' safety window.

- **Core Value:** Institutional-grade continuous-review replenishment and context-aware demand forecasting delivered as a Zero-IT, conversational, and autonomous mobile copilot.
- **Differentiator:** Unlike enterprise ERP/MRP software (e.g., SAP, NetSuite, Blue Yonder) that requires dedicated IT staff, six-figure contracts, and manual parameter tuning, mise operates frictionlessly via POS webhooks, camera invoice OCR, and plain-English natural language SMS/voice inputs. Unlike naive moving-average inventory plug-ins, mise combines ML-based continuous-review reorder points with local external event feeds and pre-built supplier lead-time hedging.

## User Flow

1. **Merchant connects POS (Square/Shopify/Clover) in 1 tap or snaps photos of past 3 distributor invoices.** — Frictionless Onboarding & SKU Mapping
2. **mise ingests sales velocity, maps raw ingredients/products, and links local neighborhood weather and event feeds.** — Contextual Baseline Calibration
3. **POS sales deplete stock in real time; mise continuously recalculates dynamic reorder points factoring in weekend event spikes.** — Continuous Review Monitoring
4. **Stock is projected to reach its reorder point within the next week; mise autonomously drafts the vendor PO, schedules it for dispatch 7 days out, and sends an actionable SMS/Push alert with a live countdown.** — Proactive Alert & Preview Window Activation
5. **Merchant does nothing (auto-dispatches on day 7, after a 48-hour reminder) or taps SMS link to adjust quantity, switch vendor, move the date, or cancel.** — Human-in-the-Loop Verification
6. **Upon delivery, merchant snaps a photo of the paper receipt or speaks 'Harina recibida' to instantly reconcile inventory and log unit prices.** — Low-Friction Delivery Reconciliation
